# Release notes

The game follows [SemVer](https://semver.org/): `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`. While the version starts with 0, a **minor** change brings new features and a **patch** change only fixes bugs. The version shown at the bottom of Settings comes from `package.json`.

For every release: change `version` in `package.json`, add an entry here at the top, then publish. The build refuses a version without an entry. These notes are shown in the game the first time you launch each new version: they are written for the player, in English, speaking to you directly. Only numbered entries are shown.

## [0.19.0] — 2026-11-19

### Added

- **Endless Night: Automatic or Manual.** With timing mode on, you now pick your driving before starting the night, and again at each **Next round**, without losing your streak or winnings. Automatic stays the default. In the middle of a manual round, **Switch to automatic for the rest of the race** lets you finish the round by chance. The screen between rounds and the end-of-night summary show which driving you used.

## [0.18.0] — 2026-11-19

### Added

- **Loading screen.** At launch, the game first loads everything the home screen and garage need, with a progress bar: no more late images or blank cards. The rest of the artwork then downloads in the background while you play. On a slow connection, a **Skip** button shows up after a few seconds.

### Fixed

- **No more white flash at launch.** The screen stays dark from the very first moment until the logo.

## [0.17.0] — 2026-11-18

### Added

- **Livelier driving.** In manual driving, every move now has feel: countdown beeps, sparks and an exhaust flame on a **Perfect** shift, a jolt and a backfire when you miss, notes that burst on the target in corners. Chain **Perfect** moves to grow your **streak**, and clear a whole sector without a mistake for a small celebration. With reduced motion, effects stay subtle.

### Changed

- **Corners.** The arrows fall toward the target again, like in a rhythm game, with the two big left and right buttons right below.

## [0.16.0] — 2026-11-18

### Added

- **Starting countdown.** In manual driving, the race starts on "3, 2, 1, Go!": you have time to get ready before the needle climbs. In the following sectors, a shorter "Ready?" announces the start.
- **Keyboard controls.** On a computer, in manual driving, the **space bar** shifts gears and the **left and right arrows** take the corners, with no need to click.
- **Guided first move.** The first time you shift gears, then the first time you take corners in manual mode, the mechanic explains the move before the start.
- **Trades from the home screen.** If you turned on "Trading with friends (experimental)", a **Trades** button now waits for you on the home screen too.

### Changed

- **Corners redesigned.** The corner arrows now match the rev counter's style, with two big left and right buttons that are easier to tap.

## [0.15.0] — 2026-11-18

### Added

- **Manual driving (experimental).** In Settings, on the Parameters tab, turn on "Timing mode (experimental)" and "Steering in corners (experimental)", then pick **Manual** under "Driving mode" on the Race tab. During the race, shift gears at the right moment on the rev counter and take the corners in rhythm with the left and right arrows: your skill replaces luck. **Automatic** mode stays as before, and you can switch back to it mid-race.
- **Trading with friends (testing).** A first version of trading cars and credits with friends arrives behind the "Trading with friends (experimental)" setting. It is still being tested, so it may not work quite right yet.

### Changed

- **Opening boosters.** The button to flip cards now always stays in the same place, whatever messages appear around the card.

### Fixed

- **One pull, one tile.** A card pulled in a special edition no longer also adds the base Pixel 16-bit edition: you no longer get two tiles for a single pull.

## [0.14.0] — 2026-11-18

### Added

- **GarageVerse now speaks English.** In Settings, on the Parameters tab, pick **Français** or **English**: the menus, cards, car stories, help, tutorial, and these release notes switch language. Your game is kept. By default the game follows your browser's language; if you already have a game, it stays in French until you change it.

### Fixed

- **Rarity color.** The same car now shows the same rarity color in the garage and on its enlarged sheet, whatever the theme.

## [0.13.1] — 2026-11-18

### Changed

- **Versus mode is taking a break.** You can't start a new challenge for now; it will be back soon. If you already have a duel in progress, you can finish it from the home screen and collect your stake or winnings as usual.

## [0.13.0] — 2026-11-17

### Added

- **Sheet view in the garage.** On a phone, pick 4 or 5 cars per row under “Per row”: your garage shows up like a binder page, with each car's artwork, number and rarity color. Tap a thumbnail to open its sheet. Cars you're missing appear as dotted outlines.

### Fixed

- **Buttons after opening a booster.** After a bulk purchase, “Open next” was squeezed next to the other buttons. It now spans the full width, above “View garage” and “Another booster”.
- **Collection order.** Cars #100 and up were sorted between #10 and #11. They now come after #99.

## [0.12.0] — 2026-11-17

### Added

- **Buy several boosters at once.** In the shop, buy 5 or 10 boosters in one go, at the same unit price. The first one opens right away, and each opening's summary lets you open the next one without going back to the shop.

## [0.11.0] — 2026-11-17

### Added

- **Anime Edition for Japanese cars.** Fifteen Japanese cars (Supra, Skyline R34, NSX, Silvia S15, MX-5, Impreza, Lancer, Celica…) get a new 90s racing-anime illustration on a mountain pass at dusk. It comes out of boosters or unlocks from the car sheet, like the other editions. Japanese cars that were already drawn in anime style keep their artwork.

### Changed

- **Easier-to-read Specials.** Tier rewards now show the ₡ symbol, just like your wallet.

### Fixed

- **Amounts stuck on screen.** On some phones, the amount you spent (for example “−80” after an upgrade) stayed displayed over the car sheet. It now disappears when its animation ends.

## [0.10.0] — 2026-11-17

## [0.5.0] — 2026-11-16

### Added

- **Complete Solarpunk and Abandoned editions.** All 129 cars in the game now have both alternate illustrations, *Solarpunk* and *Abandoned*.
- **Rival run in three stages.** Each rival is played over three progressive races (*Stage 1 · Practice*, *Stage 2 · Qualifying*, *Stage 3 · Final*). The approach stages give receipt tickets and the final duel gives the rival's signature car on the first win.
- **Two new crates in the shop**: the **Tinkerer Crate** (75 credits, common cars and up to three everyday parts) and the **Mechanic Crate** (180 credits, five guaranteed mechanical parts).
- **Seven new collection sets** (14 sets in total): *Hot Hatch Bombs*, *Cult Vans & Wagons*, *Off-Road Rovers*, *From Daily Driver to Circuit*, *On-Screen Chases*, *Icons from Across the Rhine* and *Dolce Vita & V12*.
- **Quick part removal.** A “Remove all” button in the garage's Mechanics tab and “Remove from all cars” on a part's sheet instantly return your parts to stock so you can equip your stable.
- **Third part slot on a maxed car.** A Stage 3 car with all four workshops at level 2 can unlock a 3rd part slot (+2 potential) in the workshop of its choice.

### Changed

- **Smoother difficulty curve in Endless Night** over the first fifteen rounds, with sector-specialized opposing lineups beyond that.
- **Evolutions always visible** on the Performance and Workshop tabs of the car sheet, with an “Evolution” badge in the garage whenever a Stage 3 car can unlock its evolution.
- **End-of-duel buttons** stuck above the tab bar on small screens (320 px).

## [0.4.0] — 2026-11-16

### Added

- **Desktop version.** On a large screen, the tabs move to the top bar, race setup shows on two columns (the briefing on the left, your stable and garage on the right), the filter bar stays compact and the duel cars are centered. Nothing changes on phones or tablets.
- **Rival personalities.** The six rivals have a character, a style and tastes. Their portrait and greeting line appear on the setup screen, and they answer you at the end of the duel, whether you win, lose or pull off a perfect win.
- **“Track fit” sort** to pick the cars for a race.

### Changed

- **A single car picker.** The garage and race setup use the same filters: rarity, sort, search, category and number of cars per row. Your settings carry over from one screen to the other.
- **Smoother races.** The button to move forward (“Start race”, “Commit”, “Next sector”, “See result”, “Next round”) always stays visible at the bottom of the screen, and the page follows the current step: no more scrolling to find it.
- **New button style**, more angular and raised, for the garage filters, Specials and rivals.
- **Settings in three tabs**: Settings, Themes (collapsible groups) and Help (the whole glossary). The “?” button in the header is gone.
- **Mechanical parts.** A booster contains at most two parts. About 48% of Neon Drive packs, 55% of After Hours and 63% of Overdrive contain at least one.
- **Detailed rewards** on rivals, in the shop and at the end of a duel: credits, named booster ticket and perfect win bonus.

## [0.3.0] — 2026-11-15

### Added

- **Race Specials.** Four sets, one per sector: Start, Corners, Straight and Endurance. Each has five tiers of increasing difficulty: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Legend. They open once you've beaten the whole Neon League. The first win of a tier earns 50, 100, 160, 250 then 400 credits, and replaying it earns about 20%. Beating a Special's Legend tier gives a booster ticket, just once.
- **New editions on trial.** Solarpunk (green city, wind turbines, solar panels) and Abandoned (the car in a ruined barn) on Clio III, Peugeot 205 GTI, Supra, MX-5 NB and F40.
- **Garage.** One tap on a car opens its card. The card zooms (pinch, double tap, wheel) and moves with your finger, with the holographic tilt. Cards take the color of their rarity. Rarity and sort are set with chips.
- **“New” badge** on the cars you just pulled.
- **Undo recycling.** After recycling a duplicate, you have six seconds to undo it.
- **“Help when tapping underlined words” setting** so you don't open help by mistake.
- **Endless Night mode**, **offline mode**, and **export and import of your save**.
- **New neon logo**, new icons and **metallic packs** with a golden cover when they contain a Legendary.
- **Synthwave soundtrack and sound effects**, **driving stances** (attack, normal, management), rival score ranges and a per-sector recap.
- **102 cars seen from the side** for the sector mini-race.
- **Game version** shown at the bottom of Settings, and these release notes.

### Changed

- **Unlocking an edition** now costs credits and **two duplicates** of the card. Missing something? The sheet tells you exactly what: “You're missing 1 duplicate”, “You're missing 30 credits”. Editions already unlocked are kept.
- **The 14 Legendaries** show a large still image instead of an animated scene. Eleven are in stylized Synthwave, while the Jeep, the Interceptor and the 1000tipla keep their style. 16-bit pixel art remains an edition you can pick.
- **Themes** are sorted into three groups in Settings: Moods, Universes and To Earn (the three themes to unlock stay grayed out with their goal).
- **Recycling a duplicate** asks for confirmation.

### Fixed

- Unavailable buttons are truly grayed out in every theme and tell you why.
- The purple outline around several cars seen from the side, and the Agera's animated backdrop that had no road.
- The “Performance” tab of the sheet was cut off on a small screen.
